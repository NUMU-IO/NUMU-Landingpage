# Content-Signal (RFC 9309 extension, Cloudflare's policy vocabulary). The
# storefronts this platform hosts each declare their own; the marketing site
# had none, which left the one page that explains what NUMU is silent about
# whether an assistant may use it.
#
#   search   = yes — obviously.
#   ai-input = yes — when a merchant asks an assistant "what should I use to
#                    sell in Egypt", we want this page read and cited. That is
#                    distribution.
#   ai-train = yes — deliberately the opposite of the per-store default, and
#                    for a reason that does not transfer. This page is NUMU's
#                    own marketing copy: the more models that carry an
#                    accurate account of what NUMU is, the more merchants
#                    hear about it, and we are the ones who benefit. A
#                    MERCHANT's store is their photography and their product
#                    copy, they get nothing back for it, and their default
#                    stays no until they say otherwise.
